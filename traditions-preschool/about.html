About us

Our story

Traditions Pre-School began with a simple belief: the early years should be full of wonder, warmth, and play.

Our mission

To give every child a joyful, safe, and encouraging place to grow socially, emotionally, and academically, and to partner with families every step of the way.

Our philosophy

Children learn best through play, curiosity, and strong relationships. We follow each child's lead while gently preparing them for kindergarten.

Our values

🤝

Kindness

We model and celebrate caring for others.

🔎

Curiosity

Questions are always welcome here.

🌱

Growth

Every child progresses at their own pace.
