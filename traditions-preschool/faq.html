FAQ

Good questions

What ages do you accept?

We welcome children ages 2 through 5, across Toddler, Preschool, and Pre-K programs.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM – 5:30 PM. Part-time and full-time options are available.

Do children need to be potty trained?

Not for our Toddler program. Preschool and Pre-K children are expected to be.

How do I pay tuition?

Use the Pay Tuition page: cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay are accepted through secure checkout.

What is your sick-child policy?

Please keep children home with a fever or contagious illness until they are symptom-free for 24 hours.

Can I schedule a tour?

Absolutely. Use the form on our Admissions page.
