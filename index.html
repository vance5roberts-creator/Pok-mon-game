What This Site Is

This is where I keep a running account of two separate practices: the stock ideas I publish on Substack, and the sports bets I place and log, each judged on the same terms — recorded when the decision is made, not reshaped afterward, and measured against a plain benchmark so the results can be read for what they are.

Around that core sits the rest of what I'm building: the tools and people I work with, and a small clothing line that grew out of the same interest in markets. None of it is presented as advice. It's a transparent log of the work and how it's actually performing.

About Me

I'm a 33 year old married auto assembler, with three sons. I do not have certifications of any kind. What I have instead is conviction that came from years of sitting with the work, running the same strategy through back tests, and paying attention to what held up and what did not.

This site is the one place I put the work. Some of it is mine alone. Some of it was built with other people. You will find the notes, the charts, the frameworks, the pieces that made it far enough to share, and the clothing I sell.

The platform will also serve as a home for podcasts, original media, a marketplace for stock market themed merchandise, gear, and memes, and a growing range of collaborations. The broader vision is to create an evolving ecosystem where market research, sports analytics, entertainment, commerce, and collaborative content can exist under one platform and continually build upon one another.
