About Me

Not Here To Sell You Confidence.

No certifications. No guru act. Just years of sitting with the work and writing down what actually holds up.

I'm a 33 year old married auto assembler, with three sons. I do not have certifications of any kind. What I have instead is conviction that came from years of sitting with the work, running the same strategy through back tests, and paying attention to what held up and what did not.

This site is the one place I put it all — market research, sports betting strategy, the charts and frameworks that made it far enough to share, and the merch I sell. Some of it is mine alone, some was built with other people, and it's all building toward one evolving ecosystem where research, entertainment, commerce, and collaboration feed into each other.
